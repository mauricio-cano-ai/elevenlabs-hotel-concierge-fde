# Customer Discovery

## Customer
Independent/boutique hotels in Mexico, roughly 30–100 rooms, with a direct booking engine and a front desk that cannot answer every call during peaks or after hours.

## Observed business problem
A missed reservation call is a revenue opportunity that may disappear immediately. The initial product should not ask a hotel to replace reception; it should recover overflow and after-hours intent and hand staff a qualified next action.

## V1 jobs to be done
- answer factual hotel questions in Spanish or English;
- capture dates and party size;
- verify inventory instead of hallucinating availability;
- produce a deterministic quote;
- capture guest contact/intent;
- send a booking-flow link;
- escalate explicit staff requests and system failures.

## Deliberate exclusions
No card collection, reservation mutation, or autonomous cancellation. These increase operational and compliance risk without being necessary to prove the value hypothesis.

## Pilot metrics
Recovered booking intents, booking-link conversion, qualified leads, escalation rate, latency/error rate, and zero prohibited claims/actions.
